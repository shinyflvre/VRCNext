# **2027.2.5**

## Changes
* You can now set content tags like Sexually Suggestive or Graphic Violence on your own avatars in the avatar details.
* Added **Bulk Content Tag** to the edit mode in **Avatars** to set content tags on several avatars at once.

## Fixed Bugs
* Fixed **Time Spent** showing fewer joins and less time for groups than the timeline. Groups now count all visits, including older ones.
* Fixed **FrameShot** freezing during GIF and video recording, with the frame staying red or orange.
* Fixed **FrameShot** photos, GIFs and videos taking minutes to appear.
* Fixed the whole app becoming slow after a **FrameShot** error.
* **FrameShot** now recovers on its own after a graphics error instead of needing a restart.
* Fixed GIF and video recording switching on and off when both buttons are pressed together.
* Photos from **FrameShot** are saved faster.
